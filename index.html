Why Officiating Matters

Officiating is far more than reading a rule book — game officials are responsible for building the public’s confidence in sports and are critical to the well-being of every contest, making sure games are played fairly, within the spirit of the rules, and safely. That responsibility asks for real preparation, ongoing study, and personal integrity: honoring every assignment, staying impartial, and putting an injured player’s welfare above everything else in the moment.
